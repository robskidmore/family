# AGPL-3.0 with no closed features

The repo is public, and we want a paid Hosted service. We license everything under AGPL-3.0 and keep no features closed. The Hosted service sells running the app for you: backups, email delivery, uptime and updates. It does not sell extra features. AGPL stops a third party from running a closed, modified copy as a competing service. This is the model Plausible and Immich use.

## Considered options

- MIT: lets anyone resell a closed fork of the hosted product.
- Open core (AGPL core plus closed paid modules): needs a second private codebase. It also makes self-hosters second-class, and they are who the product is for.
